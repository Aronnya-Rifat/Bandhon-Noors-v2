# Current Known Issues

## 1. Product Data Contract

Priority: HIGH

Problem:

Backend response and frontend types are not fully synchronized.

Affected:

- Product listing
- Product filters
- Product details
- Related products

Required:

Create one final product response structure.

Required fields:

```text
id
product_code
name
description
price

category_id
category_name

subcategory_id
subcategory_name

thumbnail_url

variants[]
media[]
stock information
```

Do not build further product features until this is stable.
